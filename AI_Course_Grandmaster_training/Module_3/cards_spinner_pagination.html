3. Quick Reference

Component Required Syntax / Classes
Card Outer Container <div class="card">
Card Body / Title / Text .card-body, .card-title, .card-text
Card Header / Footer .card-header, .card-footer
Border Spinner <div class="spinner-border text-primary" role="status">
Growing Spinner <div class="spinner-grow text-success" role="status">
Small Spinner .spinner-border-sm or .spinner-grow-sm
Pagination Wrapper <ul class="pagination"> inside <nav>
Pagination Items <li class="page-item"><a class="page-link" href="#">
Pagination States .page-item.active and .page-item.disabled
Pagination Alignment .justify-content-center or .justify-content-end on .pagination
