9. Quick Cheat Sheet

Component / Feature How to do it in BS5
Jumbotron <div class="p-5 mb-4 bg-body-tertiary rounded-3"> (No .jumbotron class!)
Standard Button <button class="btn btn-primary">
Outline Button <button class="btn btn-outline-success">
Large / Small Button btn-lg or btn-sm
Full-Width Button Wrap button in <div class="d-grid"> (No .btn-block!)
Button Group <div class="btn-group">...</div>
Disabled Button Add disabled attribute to <button>
