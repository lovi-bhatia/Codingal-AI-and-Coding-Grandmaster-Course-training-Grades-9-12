<div class="container bg-success">
container → I have margins and change width by breakpoint

6. The Full-Bleed Trick (container inside container-fluid)

Full-width background, but the content stays centered and readable. This is the classic pattern.

<div class="container-fluid bg-dark"> <div class="container"> ...

Section Title

The outer fluid div paints the background edge-to-edge. The inner container keeps this text aligned with the rest of the page.
