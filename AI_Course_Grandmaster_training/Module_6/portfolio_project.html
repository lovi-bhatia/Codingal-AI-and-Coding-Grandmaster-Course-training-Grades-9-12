About

A quiet obsession with the small stuff.

I started as a self-taught developer in 2017 after getting frustrated with a login form. Eight years later, I still believe the best products come from teams who care about the two-pixel gap between a label and its input.

Today I split my time between design systems for early-stage startups and teaching a small front-end course in Singapore. I write about interface craft on my newsletter, which has quietly grown past 12,000 subscribers.
