🔭 Why We Want to Go to Mars

Mars is the most Earth-like planet in our solar system! Scientists send robots called rovers to drive around the planet and look for ancient signs of water or alien bacteria.

One day, humans might even build a base on Mars. To do this, astronauts will need to figure out how to grow food and create oxygen in a frozen, dusty wasteland!

⭐ Quick Mars Facts

  • 🌡 The average temperature is minus 81 degrees!
  • 🌑 Mars has two tiny moons named Phobos and Deimos.
  • 🏃 Because of low gravity, you could jump 3 times higher on Mars.
  • 📅 A year on Mars takes 687 Earth days.
